scientific computing, and AI for Science.

functional materials, and computational modeling. I currently work on

development, experimental characterization, data analysis, and
model-assisted interpretation.

I am also interested in developing AI-enabled tools for scientific
research, including information retrieval, human–AI decision support,
and data-driven scientific workflows.
